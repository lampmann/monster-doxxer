Did anyone call it something?

If your DM used a name — even one you didn't recognise as a creature — put it here. Spelling doesn't matter; you heard it, you didn't read it. This is the strongest single thing you can tell the tool.

What did it do?

Describe what you saw, in your own words. This is the strongest evidence the tool has — far stronger than any stat. What kind of damage it dealt belongs in How did it fight? below, where it can be tied to the attack that dealt it.

What did it look like?

Your words, not the book's. This can only ever help a monster's score — it never counts against one, because reflavouring is exactly the case where the description is wrong and the monster is right.

How did it fight?

One row per thing it did — each row is matched against a single action on the statblock, so “a melee attack that burned me” finds creatures whose claw deals fire, not every dragon with a breath weapon. Fill in what you remember and leave the rest blank; a blank field costs a monster nothing.

Did it cast anything?

Name the spells you recognised. These are held loosely on purpose: a GM re-preparing a caster's spells is the most ordinary thing in the game, so a spell the statblock lacks barely counts against it. How it cast is a different matter — that comes from what the creature is, and it is weighed in full.
